# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a project about small games; nothing here is worth being
unpleasant to another person over.

## What's expected

- Be welcoming and patient, particularly with people who are new to this.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume the other person meant well, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that "no" is a complete
  answer.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including in game content contributed here.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere
else the project is being represented.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening an issue, or privately to the repository owner
through GitHub if the issue tracker isn't appropriate. Reports will be handled
discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments
that don't follow this document, and may block repeat offenders.
